Anika Tabassum Sejuty

Computer Vision & Deep Learning Researcher — Medical Imaging

I'm an M.S.–Ph.D. student at Ewha Womans University, working at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and medical ultrasound imaging. My core research focuses on understanding how blood vessels move and deform over time, but my background spans further. From image and video coding to general-purpose deep learning, machine learning, and image & signal processing across different data types. I'm drawn to problems that sit at the boundary of rigorous engineering and real clinical impact.

Research Focus

What I work on

  • Deep learning–based segmentation of vascular structures from ultrasound data
  • Subpixel level motion tracking of vessel walls using Siamese neural networks, including complex-valued representations
  • Comparative analysis & utilization of data representations (Modulated & demodulated I/Q, RF, B-mode)
  • Development and evaluation of complex-valued network architectures for signal processing
